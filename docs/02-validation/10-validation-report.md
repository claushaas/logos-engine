# Validation Report

This report synthesizes the current Validation phase: findings, evidence, decisions, risks, unresolved assumptions, and the recommendation for what should happen next.

## Executive Summary

LOGOS Engine is **not yet externally validated**. The Foundation and Validation documents are internally coherent enough to continue validation, but there is no user interview evidence, behavioral evidence, quantitative evidence, market evidence, pricing evidence, or completed experiment result yet.

The recommendation is: **continue validation**.

Confidence in this recommendation is **medium** for continuing validation and **insufficient** for proceeding to Product, Engineering, Go-to-market, pricing, or business-model execution as if the idea were validated.

The most important positive finding is internal coherence: the old project material, Foundation docs, Validation Strategy, Hypotheses, Research Plan, Experiments, Evidence Log, and Decision Record now form a traceable validation system. The most important remaining risk is that the core problem may not be painful enough for real users to change behavior, adopt the workflow, or pay for it.

## Refuted Claims

No claims have been refuted by evidence.

Absence of evidence is not refutation. The project should not stop, pivot, or narrow solely because external research has not yet been run.

Potential future refutations are already defined:

- Users do not recognize project-start ambiguity.
- Users experience ambiguity but do not care enough to change behavior.
- Current alternatives already solve the problem well enough.
- Decision-derived documents are not clearer than generic AI prose or notes.
- AI-led intake creates false authority or untraceable output.
- Diagnostics are too noisy or unactionable.
- Local-first setup blocks adoption.
- Willingness to pay does not appear after value is demonstrated.

These are not current findings. They are future decision conditions.

## Recommendation

The recommendation is: **continue validation**.

Do not proceed to Product, Engineering, Go-to-market, pricing, or business-model execution as if the idea has been validated.

### Rationale

The project has:

- a coherent Foundation;
- explicit assumptions;
- a falsifiable hypothesis register;
- a research plan;
- pre-registered experiments;
- an evidence log;
- a decision record;
- economic and business model hypotheses.

The project does not yet have:

- user problem evidence;
- audience evidence;
- behavior evidence;
- artifact comparison evidence;
- prototype or concierge workflow evidence;
- diagnostic actionability evidence;
- local-first trust evidence;
- market evidence;
- willingness-to-pay evidence;
- completed experiment results.

### Conditions

Validation may continue under these conditions:

- Start with EXP-01 and EXP-02.
- Ask problem and audience questions before solution exposure.
- Log raw evidence separately from interpretation.
- Preserve contradictory and negative evidence.
- Do not accept or refute hypotheses without traceable evidence.
- Keep pricing and business-model validation deferred until value evidence exists.
- Treat Foundation boundaries as validation constraints.

### Approval

The founder/user is the decision owner for moving from this report into actual evidence collection.

### Allowed Next Workstream

Allowed next workstream: execute the first validation research batch and update Evidence Log and Decision Record.

Blocked workstreams: product commitment, final engineering scope, GTM launch, pricing, sales, hosted collaboration, enterprise, marketplace, and broad profile expansion.
