# App Business Question Sets

## Question Set 1 — Foundation

Purpose: clarify the initial idea.

Questions:

1. What is the app idea?
2. Who is the primary user?
3. What problem does it solve?
4. How does the user solve this today?
5. Why does this matter now?
6. What would make this app meaningfully better than current alternatives?
7. What is the smallest valuable version?

## Question Set 2 — Market

Purpose: understand opportunity and context.

Questions:

1. What market or category does this belong to?
2. Who are the direct competitors?
3. Who are the indirect competitors?
4. What alternatives are users already paying for?
5. Is this a frequent or occasional problem?
6. Is the market growing, stable, or declining?
7. What niche could be served first?

## Question Set 3 — Business Model

Purpose: clarify value capture.

Questions:

1. Will this be free, paid, freemium, subscription, one-time, or B2B?
2. Who pays?
3. Why would they pay?
4. What price range seems plausible?
5. What ongoing value justifies recurring revenue?
6. What features might be paid?
7. What should remain free, if anything?

## Question Set 4 — Economics

Purpose: test business viability.

Questions:

1. What are expected fixed monthly costs?
2. What are expected variable costs per user?
3. Will AI or external APIs create variable costs?
4. What is the expected ARPU?
5. What churn assumption should be used initially?
6. What acquisition channels are expected?
7. What CAC range seems plausible?

## Question Set 5 — Product

Purpose: define product scope.

Questions:

1. What is the core user loop?
2. What is the activation moment?
3. What features are essential for MVP?
4. What features are explicitly out of scope?
5. What should the user accomplish in the first session?
6. What must be true for the app to be retained?
7. What is the main product risk?

## Question Set 6 — Design

Purpose: define user experience.

Questions:

1. What are the primary screens?
2. What is the onboarding flow?
3. What empty states are important?
4. What is the tone of the interface?
5. What accessibility constraints matter?
6. What should the app avoid visually or behaviorally?

## Question Set 7 — Architecture

Purpose: define technical direction.

Questions:

1. What platforms will be supported first?
2. What stack will be used?
3. Is the app server-first, offline-first, or hybrid?
4. What data must be stored?
5. Does the app require authentication?
6. Does it require sync?
7. What integrations are required?
8. What observability is required?

## Question Set 8 — Implementation

Purpose: define execution.

Questions:

1. What is the first milestone?
2. What should be implemented before UI?
3. What must be tested from day one?
4. What CI/CD is required?
5. What environments are needed?
6. What release process should exist?

## Question Set 9 — Go-To-Market

Purpose: define distribution.

Questions:

1. How will users discover the app?
2. What is the primary launch channel?
3. What content should exist before launch?
4. Will there be a waitlist?
5. What promise should the landing page communicate?
6. What early audience can be reached?

## Question Set 10 — Operations

Purpose: define sustainability.

Questions:

1. How will support work?
2. How will bugs be reported?
3. What metrics matter after launch?
4. What incidents are plausible?
5. What costs need monitoring?
6. How will user feedback be processed?
