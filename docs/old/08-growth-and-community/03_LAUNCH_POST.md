## Body

Most app projects fail before a single line of code is written — not because the idea was bad, but because the thinking was incomplete.

You started with enthusiasm. You built fast. Then you hit:

- "Who is this actually for?"
- "How do we make money?"
- "What does the MVP look like?"
- "What's the tech stack?"
- "When do we launch?"

Most founders answer these questions in scattered notes, Slack threads, and late-night brain dumps. The result: misalignment, preventable pivots, and wasted time.

**LOGOS Engine** is an open-source, local-first TUI that helps you turn an app idea into complete product, business, architecture, implementation, and launch documentation — before you start building.

### What It Does

1. **AI-Led Intake** — The engine conducts a focused conversation across the areas that matter: ideation, market, business model, pricing, product, UX, architecture, implementation, testing, marketing, launch, and operations.

2. **AI Reasoning** — Configure any LLM provider (OpenAI, Anthropic, Ollama, local models). AI asks follow-up questions, extracts decisions from your answers, identifies gaps, and drafts document sections.

3. **Decision Registry** — Every key decision is tracked with source, status, and dependencies. Decisions are confirmed by you — never by the AI.

4. **Canonical Documentation** — Generate 30+ Markdown documents following a consistent structure. Every document includes assumptions, open questions, risks, and next actions.

5. **Validation & Diagnostics** — Deterministic checks verify completeness. AI-assisted diagnostics flag gaps and recommend next steps.

### Why a TUI?

- Local-first, no SaaS dependency
- Git-friendly — all state is text-based
- Works in your terminal alongside your editor
- Open source, MIT licensed

### Quick Start

```bash
pnpm install && pnpm build
logos
/init
```

### How It Differs

- **Not a document generator** — it's a decision clarification engine
- **Not AI-washing** — AI is optional, always advisory, and never confirms decisions without you
- **Not a SaaS** — runs locally, writes to your repo, works offline
- **Not a black box** — every prompt, every decision, every validation rule is inspectable
