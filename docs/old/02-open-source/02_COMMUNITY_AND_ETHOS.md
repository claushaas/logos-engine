# Community and Ethos

## Core Ethos

LOGOS Engine exists to help people think before they build.

It should respect builders who are early, uncertain, learning, experimenting, or operating without institutional support.

## Community Tone

The community should be:

- rigorous;
- generous;
- practical;
- non-gatekeeping;
- anti-hype;
- focused on useful artifacts.

## What the Project Should Encourage

- documenting decisions;
- naming assumptions;
- reducing ambiguity;
- sharing templates;
- publishing examples;
- improving reasoning;
- helping others build responsibly.

## What the Project Should Avoid

- hustle culture;
- fake certainty;
- growth-at-all-costs;
- AI hype;
- premature monetization;
- opaque scoring;
- manipulative business advice.

## Educational Position

LOGOS Engine should be usable by beginners but valuable to advanced builders.

It should not dumb down important concepts.

Instead, it should structure them clearly.
