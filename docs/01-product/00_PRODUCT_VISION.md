# Product Vision

## Vision

LOGOS Engine should become the default local-first tool for people who need to think clearly before building.

It should help founders, developers, creators, consultants, and technical builders transform an idea into a coherent body of documentation.

## Product Category

Potential category:

```text
Intent-to-Documentation Engine
```

Alternative category:

```text
Structured Venture and Project Design TUI
```

## Primary User

The initial user is a technical builder who wants to create an app-based business and prefers working in:

- terminal;
- Git;
- Markdown;
- IDE;
- local repositories.

## Secondary Users

Future users may include:

- indie hackers;
- product managers;
- startup founders;
- agencies;
- consultants;
- creators;
- course builders;
- open-source maintainers;
- internal innovation teams.

## Primary Job To Be Done

When I have an idea for an app or digital product, I want a structured system to interrogate the idea and generate complete documentation, so I can reduce preventable ambiguity before investing heavily in design and development.

## Product Promise

LOGOS Engine helps you know what you are building, why it should exist, how it works, how it reaches users, and what must be true for it to succeed.

## Product Anti-Promise

LOGOS Engine does not guarantee success.

It reduces avoidable confusion.

## Product Personality

- precise;
- structured;
- calm;
- direct;
- rigorous;
- non-hype;
- transparent.
