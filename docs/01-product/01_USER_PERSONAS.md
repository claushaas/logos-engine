# User Personas

## Persona 1 — Technical Founder

### Profile

A developer or technical builder with product ideas.

### Pain

They often start building before fully clarifying:

- market;
- monetization;
- UX;
- technical constraints;
- launch strategy.

### Desired Outcome

A complete project documentation package before implementation.

## Persona 2 — Indie Hacker

### Profile

A solo builder creating small software businesses.

### Pain

They need fast, structured thinking without enterprise process overhead.

### Desired Outcome

A practical plan that covers product, business, and implementation.

## Persona 3 — Product-Oriented Developer

### Profile

A developer who wants to build apps but lacks a formal product discovery process.

### Pain

They can build software, but struggle to define what should be built and why.

### Desired Outcome

Guided product and business reasoning.

## Persona 4 — Consultant or Agency

### Profile

Someone who scopes software projects for clients.

### Pain

Clients often bring vague ideas and incomplete requirements.

### Desired Outcome

A structured intake process that produces client-ready documentation.

## Persona 5 — Creator or Educator

### Profile

Someone who wants to package knowledge into a course, community, app, or product.

### Pain

They need structure before execution.

### Desired Outcome

A reusable thinking and documentation framework.
